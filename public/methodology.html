APARADH · METHODOLOGY & SOURCES

How this was built

Every number on APARADH traces to a table in the source PDFs. This page is the receipt.

1 · The source

All crime figures come from the National Crime Records Bureau (Ministry of Home Affairs), Crime in India 2024 — the latest edition, released 6 May 2026. State/UT tables were taken from Volume I (IPC/BNS crimes) and Volume II (special-law and victim-group chapters):

  • Volume I — 604 pages, 17.8 MB
  • Volume II — 574 pages, 14 MB

No login, CAPTCHA, or paywall stands in front of these files — they are direct public downloads from ncrb.gov.in.

3 · How it was parsed — and checked

  • Extraction with pdfplumber, driven by table-aware column maps (script: scripts/parse_cii2024.py in the repo). The 2024 edition straddles two criminal codes, so tables carry IPC + BNS + Total columns — both splits are stored.
  • Integrity check on every table: the 36 parsed state/UT rows were summed and compared against the NCRB's own TOTAL STATE(S), TOTAL UT(S) and TOTAL ALL INDIA rows. All checks passed on all 24 tables; zero rows dropped, zero figures estimated.
  • Spot-checked against published national figures: 58,85,867 total crimes, 27,049 murders, 4,41,534 crimes against women, 1,01,928 cybercrimes, 55,698 vs SCs, 9,966 vs STs, 5,737 dowry deaths — all match.
  • One layout quirk handled explicitly: the NCRB wraps "D&N Haveli and Daman & Diu" across lines in four different ways across tables; the parser joins all four variants deterministically (never by guessing).

4 · The map

  • State/UT polygons: geoBoundaries IND-ADM1, sourced from the DataMeet India community / Election Commission of India — a Survey-of-India-based dataset. Country outline: geoBoundaries IND-ADM0 (bbox-verified identical). Licence: CC BY 2.5 IN.
  • The outline shown is the official Indian boundary (PoK within the J&K boundary, Ladakh as a separate UT). Geometry simplified for the web (39 KB); five tiny UTs get clickable marker dots in addition to their true polygons.
  • Choropleth bands are quintiles — five equal-count groups of the 36 states/UTs — computed live from the selected head's rates. Flat fills, no gradients.

5 · What this is not

  • Registered crime ≠ committed crime. NCRB counts FIRs. States differ in how readily police register cases, so cross-state comparisons need the NCRB's own caution (printed in every table): states/UTs may not be compared purely on crime figures.
  • Rates use NCRB's mid-year projected populations (Report of the Technical Group on Population Projections). Different heads use different denominators — the rate base is labelled on every figure.
  • Not yet parsed: the remaining ~90 minor IPC/BNS sub-heads of table 1A.4 (variable sub-column layouts, e.g. kidnapping sub-categories) and the metropolitan-city tables. Deliberately excluded rather than half-parsed.
  • 2024 figures straddle the IPC→BNS transition (new codes in force from 1 July 2024); the IPC/BNS split is shown per state where the table provides it.

6 · Reproduce it

The public repo contains the parser, the map builder, the raw download script references, and this site's source. Re-running scripts/parse_cii2024.py against the two NCRB PDFs regenerates data/aparadh_2024.json byte-for-byte (modulo PDF text-extraction versions).
